## 3. SHIPC: report it, do not gate on it

**What S1 does now.** PASS needs A1 and A2 against the app's rules and S1: CANDC's survival less SHIPC's, its lower
end above -0.1 points. SHIPC is the couples' rollout on tables at the product's settings.

**Why it should not decide.**
- No couple has ever run on SHIPC. Couples run on the app's rules (D42). For a single, "no harm against the product's
  settings" protects what users have today. For a couple it protects nothing anyone has.
- As a gate it can only take the candidate away. If CANDC beats the rules by the pilot couples' 9 to 10 points and
  sits 0.2 below SHIPC, the run is NOT PASSED and couples keep the rules at 85%. That is the wrong outcome on the
  run's own evidence.
- What it does tell you is worth having: whether the candidate's settings suit couples as well as the old ones. That
  is a finding to investigate, not a reason to withhold.

**What I would do.** Keep the arm, shorten it, and move S1 from the decision rule to "reported, not deciding":
- SHIPC runs the first 1,000 paths of each unit, in one chunk, in the first stage only.
- The pooled CANDC less SHIPC is reported at each weight with its interval. Over 40 couples at 1,000 paths its
  standard error is about 0.07 points (the sizing file's pilot figure of 0.29 to 0.30 for 2 couples, scaled).
- D34's per-couple flags against SHIPC go: 1,000 paths cannot carry them.
- If the pooled figure's interval lies wholly below -0.25 points, that is logged in the register with an owner.

This changes the question the registration asks ("...and with no harm against the couples' solver at the product's
settings", from PART-C-PLAN.md), so it is the maintainer's ruling, and the design review reads the revised text.

**What SHIPC costs.** As registered it is 70 of the first stage's 187.9 core-hours. Item 11 makes its path 1.6 to 1.9 times
faster (`results/out-couple-flat-ship-30-*.txt`), less than CANDC's 2 to 6: at the product's settings each person's
ranking still scores every move again, because those tables carry no switch charge and item 2's shortcut is taken
only where there is one. On 5,000 paths SHIPC would then be 43 of the first stage's 87 core-hours, half the run. On
1,000 paths it is 16 of 60.

Extending item 2's shortcut to a table with no charge is a few lines and an identity test (`rankActions` is the same
sum). It is only worth doing if SHIPC stays at 5,000 paths.
