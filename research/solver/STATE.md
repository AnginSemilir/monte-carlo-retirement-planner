## 1. The product question

Does the optimising solver, its tables executed by the app's own engine, give a UK household better outcomes than the app's current rules (survival first, then spending and the estate), with no material harm to any household? The
research candidate is confirmed against the shipped settings once, on a held-out seed (7u-F), and then meets the app's rules head to head (Phase 4) before anything changes in the app. Until then the app's defaults do not move.
