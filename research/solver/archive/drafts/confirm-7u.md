## Reasons

- **Survival (1, 5).** On the 25 the records pass every household at both weights, the closest wealth x2 (0 saved/3 lost

  lower end past the margin by chance. The broad 30 are new: most are not bridge households and should sit near full
  survival in both arms (no discordant path reads no material harm), but the four long-bridge ones (S364, S380, S394,
  S408) and the early-bridge four are where the candidate acts and are unseen. One harm in 55 at either weight is the
  main risk. Judged at each weight: HELD 0.68, INCONCLUSIVE 0.22, FALSIFIED 0.10.
- **Whole score (2, 6).** The records' least points were near the line at both weights (wealth x2, S120, S168), within a
  few hundredths of zero, and every interval passed. The broad 30 add households above the estate cap (S190, S214, S262,
  S394, wealthy) where the candidate de-risks toward the cap at 0.02 (O124) and pays in the whole score's rest. Judged
  at 0.02: HELD 0.70, INCONCLUSIVE 0.20, FALSIFIED 0.10; at 0.01: HELD 0.75, INCONCLUSIVE 0.17, FALSIFIED 0.08.
- **Spending (3, 7).** The records' least household was S370 at about -1.4% at both weights, far inside -5%, and the panel
  mean well above -1%. Judged at each weight: HELD 0.88, INCONCLUSIVE 0.08, FALSIFIED 0.04.
- **The floor (4, 8).** The 17 no-gain households of the 25 mostly gain a little in the records (27 to 41 saved and none
  lost on several), and the broad 30 add paths with few discordances; the floor needs a pooled loss near 0.1 points to
  fail. Judged at each weight: HELD 0.88, INCONCLUSIVE 0.08, FALSIFIED 0.04.
