# O111: where the pauses sit

## The deep review after PAUSE-S128 (deep-review-log.md 6 Oct 04:34 UK), FLAG 1

O111's counts, and PAUSE-S128's LOC and FIRST (results-pause128.txt), take a pause to be a year in which the used share u grows by under 0.01 (audit-pause.mjs, from derive-hyb-edges.mjs flatYears). That is the hold after the State Pension: a zero draw, since the State Pension fills the personal allowance. Before the State Pension the hold is a draw that fills only the personal allowance, which grows u by 12,570 / 0.75 x 0.25 / 268,275 = 0.0156 a year (the review's arithmetic, grade A), above the 0.01 bound, so it was never counted. The row's reading of where the flat years sit stands; it is never quoted as the whole hold.
