## In plain words

The savings split lets each working year's move choose where that year's saving goes, at the same net cost to the
household:
- the plan's own saving;
- the pension first (its tax relief taken), then the ISA;
- the ISA first.

Without the split, the plan's own saving stands. This run solved Phase 4's 27 households that save into a pension or an
ISA twice, with and without the split (CANDS, CANDD), on today's candidate, on the same 4,000 simulated markets. Every
household took the candidate's own menu, the solve on anchors.

**In retirement the two arms run one policy.** This write-up first said the opposite, from `B7-FACTORIALS.md` item 2. That
was measured on the windfall split's copies, and it does not hold here (the reviewer at the read, finding 1):
- On anchors a copy can reach a year without a choice in one way only: the move a cell stored the year after
  (`solve.js` `coarseMask`). That happens only where a year without a choice comes before a year with one, as the years
  before a windfall's deposit year do.
- On every household here the years with a choice run without a gap from its first year to the year before it retires.
- The probe after the read (`save-skip-probe.mjs`, `results-save-skip.txt`; EXPLORATORY, no path run) solved both arms of
  all 27 households as the run solved them, at 45 points on anchors:
  - on every one, no year without a choice comes before a year with one;
  - outside the working years, the same to the bit in both arms: each year's tables, the bridge reader's tables (on the
    20 households it reads, in their years 15 to 20) and its adaptive reference.
  - Its control, the windfall split on S026, differs in the years before its deposit year, as `B7-FACTORIALS.md` item 2
    found.
  - The reviewer's own probes found the same on S188, S308F and S244 (`reviews/2026-11-19-read-save.md`).
- So CANDS and CANDD part in retirement only through the state each brings to it.

  choices and what follows from them, not the skip's. There is no skip's part to measure.
- **What a v2 would still settle:**

  - **The engine's ISA defect** (`reports/2026-11-18-windfall-read.md`). It reaches this panel on one household:
    - S310F, whose own policy puts its windfall into its ISA in a retired year in both arms;
    - the split's own ISA route goes through the year's regular contributions, which are counted
      (`results-save-isa-deposits.txt`);
    - its effect on S310F is unmeasured, but the read bounds its part (`results-save-beside.txt`, section 6). S310F is not
      among the households the verdict names (its survival gain's Holm `5.78e-1`, its spending gain's `6.20e-2`). Without
      it the panel stays beyond noise: survival `+0.399` points (se `0.0266`), spending `+0.4341%` (se `0.0049`). So no
      gain the verdict names rests on the defect.
    - Fixing it before a v2 is still recommended: the engine on `main` carries it.
- **The agent's recommendation:** a v2 test of the savings split on the whole menu, after the defect is fixed. The harm on
  S308F should be looked at in it: one of the five households saving ISA first, its survival level, its spending down a
