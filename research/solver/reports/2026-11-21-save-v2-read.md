## Beside the rule, after the read (`results-save-v2-beside.txt`; the reviewer's MINORs 1 to 3)

- **The time (MINOR 1).** With the split, a working saver's path takes about twice as long.
  - Each arm's CPU a path on anchors, CANDSA over CANDDA: the median `x2.10` (`x1.84` to `x2.31`). That is `x1.90` with five
    working years to choose and `x2.14` with fifteen. On the whole menu: `x2.08`.
  - The solve on anchors: the median `x1.10` (`x0.65` to `x1.46`), a guide only, since four units ran at a time.
  - The cause, from the code (`src/solver/solve.js`): a menu carrying the split's copies sets `r.nAt`. With it set, the
    chooser drops P1's once-a-decision memo and the shortlist in every year of a path, not only in the working years.
  - So the speed mode, which is the shortlist alone, does nothing on a working saver's path once the split is on. The page's
    "faster" would not hold for them. This is what the code does, not an inference.
- **Where the gain sits (MINOR 2).**
  - **By kind.** Of the `3,820` paths CANDS saves, `2,084` are paths that met every year's spending under CANDD but ended
    below the minimum pot; the other `1,736` ran out. Net of the `649` paths lost, `1,558` of the `3,171` gained (`49.1%`)
    are the minimum pot's: `56.3%` on the library cohort, `36.0%` on the younger.
  - **In time.** None of the library cohort's saved paths fails under CANDD in the first six retired years, and `2,001` of
    its `2,516` fail in the plan's last three. Of the younger cohort's `1,304`, `285` fail in the first six retired years
    and `656` in the last three.
  - **The younger cohort's spending gain** sits in its first five retired years: `94.68%` of target under CANDD against
    `98.32%` under CANDS. After them the two arms are within `0.3` points.
  - **By employment.** The 30 employees gain `+0.577` (`+0.540` to `+0.613`) by path, `87.3%` of the pooled gain, and
    `74.3%` of their working-year decisions fall from 2029, where the engine leaves salary sacrifice's NIC relief uncapped.
    The ten self-employed, whose relief is income tax alone, gain `+0.252` (`+0.214` to `+0.290`), each of the ten above
    nought. So the gain does not rest wholly on the uncapped relief. How much of the employees' gain does is not measured:
    the two groups' plans differ in more than the relief.
- **The pot and the tax by household (MINOR 3).**
  - The mean pot left is lower on 11 of 40 households (7 of the younger 10) and the median on 10. The capped pot, the part
    the solver values, is lower on 6: S302F `-6.054%`, S300F `-4.046%`, S336F `-2.049%`, S354F `-0.645%`, S330F `-0.346%`

  - Lifetime tax is higher on 34 of 40 households. It counts each year's `taxPaid` and `cgtPaid`, and `taxPaid` leaves out
    the tax on the pay. So a working year's relief on a pension saving never shows in it, while the tax on a larger
    pension's later draws does: the rise is not shown to be a cost.

## What the change of default needs (for the maintainer)

1. **The default:** `saveSplit: true` in the candidate, in `research/solver/candidate.json` and the app's `APP_CANDIDATE`
   together, with the decided-defaults block and `candidate.test.mjs` pinning the new candidate and the one before it.
2. **A couple's solves kept off the split,** with its test and plant. Since D135 these are each person's table on today's
   route, and the household's one table, both solved with a partner's age.
3. **The words:** the page says nothing yet of where a year's savings go. Its actions, the next years and the ledger name
   draws, harvests and tiers, not the save. With the split on, a working saver's plan would carry a decision the page never
   tells them. So the build adds its words:
   - this year's action;
   - the rows;
   - a skip with its cost, as each other part of a move has.

   The words are the agent's draft and the maintainer's to change. The registration does not name this step; the agent
   counts it part of the change.
4. **What the default reaches by inference only, said in the D-line.**
   - As the registration says: the dial's locked positions 3, 6 and 10; consent to change risk withdrawn; the 'lower' estate
     aim; and the years from 2029 for a salary-sacrificing employee.
   - From the panel's plans (the reviewer's MINOR 4 (a); `results-save-v2-beside.txt` section D):
     - every plan saves 12% of its salary into the pension and 6% into the ISA, rising 3% a year, and nothing else;
     - none has salary growth, employer NIC passed through or the MPAA;
     - the employees earn £29,500 to £85,000, so none is in the personal allowance's taper or at the additional rate;
     - the working years are 5 or 15; 30 plans are taxed in the rest of the UK and 10 in Scotland.

     The split reaches every working saver whatever their own saving's shape.
   - **The speed mode is not reached by inference** (MINOR 1): the code shows it does nothing on a working saver's path once
     the split is on. Its words would have to say so, or the speed mode be made to act with the split.
5. **The time goes to the maintainer before the change is committed** (MINOR 1): a working saver's path about twice as
   long, and the speed mode idle for them. It is the trade the maintainer has ruled on for a couple's path (D105, D108,
   D109). Phase 9's budgets were measured without the split.
6. **An identity on the code the change lands on** (MINOR 4 (b)). The code id is now 9f9857213b75, since the household
   default's port, against the run's 71fb4271c681. The change's commit shows the app's route for a working single, with the
   split on, equal path for path to the tested arm (CANDSA) on some of the panel's households, on seed 7002 and not the
