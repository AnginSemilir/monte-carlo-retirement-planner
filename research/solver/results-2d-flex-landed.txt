Phase 2d under the three-world mixture, clean (tag flex-landed)

The run the shelved pilot should have been: the pre-registered 41, seeds 7001/7002, 3,000 held-out
paths, three-world mixture, 30 points, levels 1.2/1.1/1/0.9/0.8, raise weight 0.003, CONF=gkFloor
(equal downside against the guardrails), margin 0.5pt, and the single-stage landing on 5,400 search
paths. Run from a frozen snapshot of 3308982, every household stamped
solverVersion 2026-09-21.single-stage-landing, so the 41 were solved by identical code.

THE LANDING, which is why this was re-run.

  short of the ask (floor < ask - 0.5)     old landing 7 of 35     new landing 0 of 41
  landing margin (floor minus ask)         min -0.27, mean +0.80, max +2.10
  below the ask at all                     4 of 41, none by more than 0.27
  above the ask by more than 2 points      1 of 41 (S292, +2.10, lambda 0.371, not the bracket floor)

Forty-one of forty-one. The old landing met its promise on 28 of the 35 clean pilot households; this
one meets it on every household, and the cost of doing so is visible: paired on the 35 the two runs
share, lambda is about 0.69 of what it was, so the solver trims roughly a third more than it did when
it was quietly under-trimming.

WHAT THAT COST, paired on the same 35:

  years at or above target (median run)    -0.037
  spending delivered (mean level)          -0.005

Both are small, and both were predicted before the run from the direction of the bias. This is the
price of the floor being kept honestly rather than a regression, and the guardrail columns do not move.

AGAINST THE GUARDRAILS AT EQUAL DOWNSIDE, 41 households:

                                        solver     GK with floor
  floor rate                            +0.73 pts over the guardrails' own
  years at or above target, median run    0.849        0.439
  spending delivered, median run          1.101        1.012
  spend-level changes per run             5.18        26.35
  median pot                            +£186k

The shape the pilot showed survives and is now measured on a solver that keeps its promise: roughly
twice the years at the full target, a fifth of the whipsaw, more spending delivered, a larger pot.
Against the guardrails as shipped (no floor to respect) the solver gives up 4.10 points of floor rate,
which is the survival those rules buy by simply spending less; against Vanguard's dynamic rule it is
+3.50 on the floor with a £472k larger pot; against ARVA +7.53.

ONE METRIC THAT GOES THE OTHER WAY, and it is worth understanding rather than burying. The
fully-funded rate (paths that are never below target in ANY year) is +13.43 for the solver on the mean
but only 22 up / 19 down, sign test p = 0.755, and on several households the solver's is 0.0 against
the guardrails' 19.2. The two rules distribute their trimming differently: the solver makes small
adjustments across most futures, so few futures are entirely untouched, while the guardrails do
nothing at all until a ratio crosses a line and then cut hard. A household is at its full target in
85% of years under the solver and 44% under the guardrails; it is far rarer under the solver to have a
retirement with no adjustment at all. Which of those a person prefers is a real preference, not a
defect, and the product copy has to say both.

THE LABEL THAT WAS WRONG. Seven households finished at the bottom of the lambda bracket and were
labelled "confidence not reachable". None had failed. Every one had an ask between 97.6 and 99.0, sat
at lambda 0.0050 after 2 solves, and delivered within 0.37 of its ask; the reducer counted all seven
as landings. What the bracket floor means is that the ask PLUS the half-point margin is more than
trimming can buy, because at that level the remaining failures are ones no amount of trimming fixes.
The label now reads "at the bracket floor" and describes where the solve stopped, leaving the pass/fail
to the caller. No behaviour changed.

COST. Mean 5.0 solves a household (max 7), mean 2,332 s, 26.6 core-hours for the 41 on four cores.
Paired on the 35 shared with the pilot, 2,369 s against 1,836 s: 1.29x, not the 2.2x measured earlier.
That earlier figure was taken with six processes fighting over four cores; 1.29x is the honest price of
5,400 search paths and the extra solves a good estimate justifies.

STILL OPEN. 5,400 was chosen because it worked, not because it is the least that works; a sweep on the
three households the old landing missed would say whether a smaller count holds. And S292's +2.10
sits right at the over-trim guard the suite asserts, at a lambda well off the bracket floor, so it is
the one household worth a look before that guard is trusted as a bound.
