The spending pilot under the mixture, first pass (tag flex-mix): SHELVED, no headline

WHY IT IS NOT A RESULT. solve.js was edited three times while this 41-household batch was in flight
(14:51, then twice more), and the batch spawns a fresh Node per household, so households that started
after each edit loaded a different solver. Classified by the SOLVER_VERSION / verifyPaths fields that
only the later code writes:

  clean, the original single-sample landing on 600 search paths:   35 households
  contaminated, one of two later solvers:                            6   S374 S378 S390 S400 S410 S414

A pre-registered set of 41 has to be solved one way. This one was not. The clean pass with the fixed
landing is tag flex-landed (`batch-flex-landed.sh`, run from a snapshot); its results file replaces
this one for every purpose.

WHAT THE 35 CLEAN HOUSEHOLDS SAY, kept only because it is the sharpest measurement of the OLD landing.
Reduced by copying the 35 uncontaminated records to a scratch tag and running reduceFlex on it; the
scratch directory was then removed. Against the guardrails at equal downside:

  floor rate                          mean +0.23 pts
  years at or above target, median    0.881 vs 0.414
  spending delivered, median run      1.102 vs 1.010
  spend-level changes per run         5.27 vs 25.13
  median pot                          +£159k
  landed the floor promise            28 of 35     (the guardrails, by construction, 35 of 35)

Twenty-eight of thirty-five. The partial reduce at 24 households had read 21 of 24, and the three
misses looked like the whole story; on the full clean set a fifth of households fell short of the
floor they were promised. That is the winner's curse at 600 search paths, diagnosed and fixed in
`results-2d-flex-landing.txt`, and it is why these comparison figures cannot stand: a solver that is
not keeping its promise is spending money it should have held back, and the years-at-target and
spending-delivered figures above are flattered by exactly that amount. Expect the clean pass to show
the solver lower on both, and the guardrails unchanged.

The shape of the comparison is not in doubt and will survive: twice the years at the full target, a
fifth of the whipsaw, more spending delivered, a larger pot. The size is what the clean pass sets.
