WHAT IT MEANS
  The maintainer's intuition (23 Sep) holds better than the toy did: in the real solver, moving up a tier happens
  only when a household is behind, and across the markets it is averaged over it helps - survival up 2-3 points on
  the thin households and fewer years without money - without the tail getting worse. The toy's tail result did
  not carry over (it had no tax, no flexible spending and one pot). It is still a move made precisely when a plan
  is in trouble, so it stays a consent question for the product, not a default.
