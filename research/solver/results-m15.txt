WHY (read off the same results, no further run)
  With the option on, the pension and ISA sit below their plan tier far less: S330 38.1 -> 9.7 years a path
  (5.3 at 40% gain), S054 6.9 -> 4.5, S390 9.5 -> 7.3. The GIA itself barely moves (S330: 0.003 years a path).
  On the joint menu, "pension and ISA one step down" now also means "the GIA one step down", and every library GIA
  already sits at Medium, two tiers under the pension. So the bundled move scores worse than the pension-only step
  it replaced, and the solver keeps the pension at High instead: the S330 estate rises (median 3.40m -> 4.63m) and
  survival falls. The "keep the GIA where it is" check scores only the move that already WON; it cannot bring back
  the pension-only step, which lost the ranking because it was bundled. The tables are solved the same way, so
  their continuation values also assume the GIA follows every future step.

WHAT A WORKING VERSION NEEDS (for the maintainer; not built)
  The GIA's choice has to be scored for every tier-changing move, not only the winner - in the forward decision
  (about twice the evaluations for the two thirds of moves that change tier) and in the tables (the max over "GIA
  follows" and "GIA stays" per move, about 1.7x the solve). That is the "independent pair" Phase 6 measured at 4-5x
  the cost for no value, in a cheaper form. Given the library's GIAs all sit at Medium, a separate GIA tier menu of
  its own is the alternative worth deriving before building either.
