CONDITION 1. Landing

  1a. floor rate at or above the ask less 0.5:  41 of 41.  PASS
      margin (held-out floor minus ask): min +0.27, mean +1.33, max +3.00

  1b. no household more than 2 points above its ask (the over-trim guard, C4b):  9 of 41 exceed. FAIL

      Seven of the nine took NO TRIMMING AT ALL: lambda pinned at the bracket top (2.0), one solve,
      zero trims. C4b's reasoning is 'lambda well above the bracket bottom means the solver CHOSE this
      level of trimming'. At the bracket TOP it chose none, and the de-risking alone lifted the floor
      above the ask. That case did not exist when the guard was written: flex-landed had 0 of 41 needing
      no trimming, flex-tiers has 18 of 41.

          S004 +2.13   S194 +2.67   S222 +2.47   S234 +2.43   S240 +2.43   S300 +3.00   S374 +2.47

      Two are genuine landings, and on the sample the landing actually optimised they are inside 2:

          S268  ask 75.1   held-out 77.83 (+2.73)   search 76.50 (+1.40)   lambda 0.946, 7 solves
          S292  ask 77.2   held-out 79.57 (+2.37)   search 77.93 (+0.73)   lambda 1.140, 7 solves

      The landing targets the 5,400 search paths; the guard is measured on the 3,000 held-out paths,
      which carry about 0.8 points of standard error at these rates. The excess is the gap between two
      samples, not spending given away. Both trim less than flex-landed did (lambda 0.946 vs 0.447,
      1.140 vs 0.371: a HIGHER lambda is LESS trimming).

      This is recorded as a failure because it is one as written. It is not re-specified here.
