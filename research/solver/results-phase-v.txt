WHAT IT MEANS
1. THE PLANS ARE STABLE; THE TABLE'S OWN NUMBERS ARE NOT. Every change of quadrature, wealth points and
   share points moved the simulated survival by no more than noise (about a point at 1,000 paths), while
   the table's reading moved by up to several points and does not settle. The policy - which move to
   make - is robust; the value the table attaches to a position is not.
2. SO THE TABLE MUST NEVER BE THE REPORTED NUMBER. Survival, spending and pots shown to a user come from
   simulation. This was already the plan since survival stopped being a target; it is now a requirement.
3. 6 TO 9% OF STORED MOVES CHANGE WITH THE SETTINGS without changing the simulated survival - consistent
   with near-ties, but 1,000 paths cannot see below about a point, and survival is the only outcome V
   measured. Spending and tax are not yet checked at the settings' extremes.
4. THE ONE REAL NUMERICAL DEFECT IS #106's DEAD CORNER, confirmed four ways and confined to households
   retired before pension access with a pension share above 0.8 (S126 of the 41). Neither interpolation
   scheme reads it well; the fix is to keep the dead node out of the read.
5. LINEAR IS NOT A SAFE REPLACEMENT FOR LOG-ODDS. It wins on average along wealth for two households and
   fails catastrophically on the third (52 points at worst). No change to the wealth-axis read.
