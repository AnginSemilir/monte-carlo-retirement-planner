## Findings

1. MINOR. P2's stated power range is slightly off at both ends; the decision-relevant top end stands for the households
   with records. The registration l.33 to 36 says 80% of the time "from a true loss of 1.03 to 1.20 points" and 90% "from
   1.04 to 1.30, the widest on S370"; STATE.md l.188 adds "less elsewhere".
   - The low end (S366, results-reread l.20 and l.36) holds the household's 7u discordance (0.17%) fixed below the loss
     tested (1.03%). That cannot happen: a true loss of L needs at least L of the paths discordant. reread-7u-item4.mjs
     l.80 to 81 evaluate pHarm(L, d) with d < L. With the discordance at least the loss (pHarm(L, max(d, L))), 80% needs
     1.068 points and 90% needs 1.106. A simulation through reduce-7uf.mjs's perHousehold agrees: SHIP 90%, every
     discordant path a loss, Holm over 28, 400 replicates read harm 0.797 at a true loss of 1.07 and 0.910 at 1.10. The
     printed figures are 0.695 at 1.03 and 0.710 at 1.04.
   - The range covers the 15 households 7u ran (reread l.89 filters to them). S362, S382, S406 and S418 have no record,
     and the P2 line, unlike the P1 line, does not say so. "The widest on S370" is therefore not established. A household
     at 15% discordance needs 1.39 points for 90%. S362's preflight read 19 of 400 paths discordant (4.75%;
     results-preflight-7uf.txt l.8), inside the range.
   - S366 sits at the band's top: SHIP 99.47% at W0.02 and 99.44% at W0.01 in 7u (reread l.20, l.36). On 7014's 16,000
     paths SHIP's own sampling se there is about 0.057 points. So S366 lands outside the band (reported, not gated, under
     D3) with chance about 0.3 at W0.02 and 0.15 at W0.01, or about 0.38 and 0.28 once 7u's own estimate error is
     counted, whatever CAND does. Its power figure holds only when it lands inside. SHIP on Step 1's code may sit
     elsewhere.
   None of this changes D3, the read or a decision. Fix, cheap: use max(d, L) in lossAt, name the four households, and add
   one clause about S366 to the Paths field. Or launch as is, knowing that "90% from 1.04" means about 1.10.

2. MINOR. P1's "HELD with chance about 0.99 at no true change" (registration l.30 to 32; results-reread l.14) is the
   optimistic end, not a bound. reread-7u-item4.mjs l.85 to 87 average the 14 recorded households over 14. The read
   averages 18. Modelled on 7u's records at 16,000 paths:
   - the four at no change and no spread: se 0.0184, HELD at no change 1.000, 80% at -0.048;
   - the four each as discordant as S370 (9.04%) and independent of the rest: se 0.0318, HELD 0.883, 80% at -0.011
     (W0.01: 0.887, -0.012). 7u's records support independence: the path-by-path correlation of CAND less SHIP between
     S370, S380, S364, S366, S394, S224, S248, S284 and S360 lies within -0.13 to 0.07.
   The rule's protection is unaffected. At a true pooled loss beyond -0.1, P(PASS) is below 0.025 by construction. What
   moves is the chance that a sound candidate reads INCONCLUSIVE on its one seed: 1% to about 12%. Fix: print the range in
   reread and quote it in the Paths field.

3. MINOR. Gaps for the time-box stop the registration provides for (l.69 to 71, the rest run "on the same snapshot"), the
   residual of the first review's 5.
   - batch-7uf.sh re-runs all 106 units and has no skip for finished ones.
   - run-from-snapshot.sh always makes a new snapshot, so "the same snapshot" can only mean the same code id.
   - read() ties each unit's JSON to the logs only through seed, paths, registration and blob (reduce-7uf.mjs l.82 to 90).
     The JSON's code and audit stamps are compared only with the household's other arm (l.58), not with the logs' single
     version (l.159).
   - batch-7uf.sh l.9 to 10 accepts a non-empty diag7uf.
   So after a full re-run under the same registration blob on changed code, a household-weight whose CAND and SHIP both
   failed in the re-run would be read from the earlier launch's files. This is narrow and touches nothing on a clean run.
   Fix before any resume, not before launch: the read requires every JSON's stamp.code and stamp.audit to equal the logs'
   version, and batch-7uf.sh skips (or a resume script runs only) the units whose JSON is missing.

4. MINOR. The registration l.50 says of a household whose engine and runPolicy differ that "any such path is listed". But
   reduce-7uf.mjs l.193 to 194 prints only the counts (the engine alone, runPolicy alone) and the words "listed, below the
