## Findings

1. BLOCKING. HOW-IT-WORKS section 4 states a measured result that results-n4.txt contradicts. Lines 133-134 say "N4
   tried the midpoints and the sale, and neither beat the menu by more than the measurement's noise". CGT (the sale) did
   beat the menu beyond noise:
   - the panel mean at W0.02 was +0.084 survival (se 0.023) and +0.091 score (se 0.023);
   - S330 gained +0.300 (se 0.087) at W0.02 and +0.350 (se 0.090) at W0.01, each with its paired 95% interval above
     zero (results-n4.txt l.9, 12, 20, 23).

   CGT failed N4's bar on size, not on noise. The bar (n4-menu.mjs l.14-16) asks for a gain beyond noise and larger than
   0.2 points on the panel mean or 0.5 on a household. Lines 136-137 ("a move joins only where it beats this one by more
   than the measurement's noise") misstate the same rule: by them CGT should have joined. The claim must not stand.
   Proposed fix: say that neither cleared N4's bar, that the midpoints were within noise, and that the sale's gain was
   beyond noise but below the bar's size, pointing at STATE.md's n4read block rather than typing figures. Then state the
   joining rule as beyond noise and above N4's targets.
