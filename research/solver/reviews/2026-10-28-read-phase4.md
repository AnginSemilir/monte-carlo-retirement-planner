     | arm | mean spend in the fixed years | path-years below target | failed by year 7 | survival |

     | FIXBR | 100.0% of target | 0.0% | 37.20% | 62.79% |

     | APP | 99.5% | 40.5% | 14.88% | 84.95% |

   - **What follows.**
     - The -36.99 on S368, and the pooled -4.294, mix arm A's draw order with taking away the candidate's spending cuts.
       Most of S368's loss is the spending.
     - Neither figure is the worth of the solver's withdrawal choices in the bridge. Neither is what the app's own rules
       do there: APP beats FIXBR by 22 points on S368.
     - 8ff8353's "the candidate following arm A's policy" should not be read that way.
     - FIXBR is reported, not deciding (D35), so no decision moves. If 7q's question (the solver's bridge handling
       against a fixed policy) matters to Part C, it needs an arm that holds the draws fixed and keeps the spend choice.
2. MINOR. **"Fewer years below target on every household" is true as counted, but it hides where and how deep the cuts
   are.**

     mean) include 4.71 years at 95 to 99%, mostly the guardrails' skipped inflation rise.
   - **CAND cuts deeper and more often to the floor.** CAND/0.02 has 10.38 years below target, nearly all of them at 90%
     or less. It spends more years at the 80% floor than APP: 8.88 against 5.96. SHIP/0.02 has 8.20.
     - On 16 of 40 households CAND/0.02 has more years at or below 90% than APP. These are the library 13 that are retired
       at the start, plus S204, S306F and S310F.
     - On 15 of 40 its summed shortfall below target is larger.
   - **Where the cuts fall:**
     - On the 13 households retired at the start (S026 to S164, S368, S384), CAND at both weights, like SHIP/0.02, spends at
       the 80% floor in the first year on every path, and at a mean of 80 to 84% in the second. APP spends 100%.
     - At retirement, S304F, S306F, S308F and S310F start at 80% on 99 to 100% of paths under CAND/0.02.
     - On S368, CAND spends a mean 83.7% of target through the eight bridge years.
   - **Timing.** A2, a lifetime sum in today's money, passes and is the registered measure. It does not show that the
     candidate's gains are bought partly with spending at the floor in the first years.
   - **The year before failure.** On failing paths, both solver arms often spend 110% of target the year before the path
     fails: 5,399 of CAND/0.02's 18,276 failures before the last year, and 6,283 of SHIP/0.02's 22,588. In the failure
     year the level reads 100% on every such path. APP fails at its 80% floor on 60,337 of its 67,761. I have not
     decomposed why; the table judging the path lost is a likely reading.
   - **For the deep review and Part C.** Both are user-visible and belong in front of the maintainer. Neither is a flag
     under D34 or D37.
