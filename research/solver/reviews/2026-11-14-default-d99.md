   every couple the solver runs.**
   - `src/solverApproximations.js` l.22: "It solves for one person. A couple's plan keeps the app's own rules." Its header
     (l.4) says each line is "a fact of the code", and l.12 cites D42.
   - D42 put the line there ("with a line saying so") while couples kept the app's rules.
   - Where it shows:
     - Details' coverage card, "What this model will not tell you", for every plan in the new planner (PlannerCards.jsx
       l.119; App.jsx l.16520 hands Details `docsSolverCard` whatever the couple's state);
     - the documentation (Docs.jsx l.70, l.199).
   - With `COUPLES` true (couplesSwitch.js l.25):
     - a couple inside the guard sees its solved plan's figures beside a statement that its plan keeps the app's rules;
     - a couple outside it reads "It solves for one person" beside a card that says the solver has been tested on couples.
   - No check reads the text: check 17 counts the list's items, `d17.approx >= 8` (solver-planner-ui.cjs l.405-408). So the
     gate passes it.
   - The commit, the D-line and PART-D D6 do not mention it.
   - To pass: the line states which couples the solver plans for (holding 40% to 60% each, or the elder entered first
     holding up to 90%) and that any other couple's plan keeps the app's own rules. The rollback build should say the old
     line, for example by reading `COUPLES`. Keep eight items so check 17 still holds.

2. **MINOR. No check runs an uneven couple's solve to its end in the page.** The part of D99 nothing tests is the worker and
