## Findings

1. **MINOR, for the maintainer with the change of default: the split doubles a working saver's path and leaves the speed
   mode idle for them.** The write-up says nothing of the time. Its list, like the registration's, gives the speed mode as
   reached "by inference only", which the code does not bear out.
   - **The figures.** Results line 103 gives a path at CANDD 46.9 ms, CANDS 96.1 ms, CANDDA 47.0 ms and CANDSA 97.1 ms.
     - **The path, by household** (each arm's `cpuUs / n`), on anchors, the menu that ships: x1.84 to x2.31, median x2.10.
       That is x1.90 with five working years and x2.14 with fifteen.
     - **The solve on anchors:** median x1.10 (x0.65 to x1.46). This is a guide only: four units ran at a time, under
       load.
   - **Why, from the code.** A menu carrying the split's copies sets `r.nAt` (solve.js 2930). The chooser then drops two
     things in every year of the path, not only in the working years:
     - P1's once-a-decision memo (`decisionRepeats`, 2244: `if (r.nAt || ...) return null`);
     - the chooser's shortlist (1855: `... && !r.nAt ? shortMask(...) : null`).

     Outside the working years the copies are already left out of the scored moves (`splitCounts`, `movesAt`). If a
     decision's cost went with its count of moves, three times the moves in five of 41 years would give about x1.25. The
     five-year households' x1.90 is far above that.
   - **So the speed mode acts nowhere in a working saver's chooser once the split is on.**
     - `SPEED_OPTIONS` is `chooserShortlist: 'order'` alone (solverSettings.js 123-126 at HEAD), and the shortlist
       touches no table: it is set on the solved plan after its tables are built (solve.js 2921-2924).
     - So such a saver's speed-mode paths are the whole menu's, which this run measured as CANDSA. They are no faster.
     - The page's "faster" (D104 (4)) would not hold for them.

     This is what the code does, not an inference.
   - **The ask:**
     - correct the speed mode's entry before the D-line is written;
     - say the time beside the outcome;
     - put the time to the maintainer before the change is committed, as the trade the maintainer has ruled on for a
       couple's path (D105, D108, D109). Phase 9's budgets were measured without the split.

     Keeping P1 and the shortlist in the years where the copies are left out is a lead for speed work. It is not measured.
